### 2. Review of Slot S1 Inputs (N1, A1, Q1)

#### 2.1 Input from Task N1 (BA Scope & Domain Inquiries — Nguyên)
* **Inquiry on Loan / Reservation Interaction:** What occurs if a book title has 0 available copies, but 1 copy is currently on hold (`Reserved`) waiting for pickup? Can another reader reserve it?
  * *Discussion:* The reservation queue is title-based. If a copy is held for Reader A, Reader B can still reserve the title, joining at position #2 in the FIFO queue.
* **Inquiry on Account Overdue Restrictions:** Should overdue accounts be suspended immediately or after a grace period?
  * *Discussion:* For a student academic project, immediate restriction upon due date expiration keeps business logic predictable and testable.

#### 2.2 Input from Task A1 (Screen Inventory & UI Architecture — Ánh)
* **Screen Decomposition:** Proposed decomposing UI into 12 screens (`SCR-01` to `SCR-12`), separating the Circulation Desk into two dedicated workflows: Borrow/Checkout (`SCR-07`) and Return/Check-in (`SCR-08`).
* *Consensus:* Approved unanimously. Splitting circulation tasks simplifies wireframes and reduces operational errors for the Librarian actor.

#### 2.3 Input from Task Q1 (Technical Skeleton & Schema Feasibility — Quân)
* **Transactional State Integrity:** Identified high risk of race conditions during concurrent checkouts and hold allocation (`R1` in PA1).
* *Recommendation:* Enforce PostgreSQL row-level locking (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`) in PA3 and document explicit state transition pre-conditions in PA2 specifications. Approved.
