**Naming authority:** The frozen Master ID Registry defines UC-09 as Renew Book Loan. The task-plan heading that says "UC-09 Overdue" conflicts with this authority. Overdue monitoring is addressed by FR-F05-07 and UC-12; no new or renamed UC identifier is introduced here.

## Goal and scope

A Reader renews their own active Loan, or a Librarian renews it on their behalf, exactly once for an additional 14 calendar days when the Loan is not Overdue and there are no pending Reservations for its Book Title.

## Trigger, preconditions and guarantees

- **Trigger:** Reader selects **Renew** on SCR-11 or Librarian selects a Loan in SCR-07.
- **Preconditions:** Authenticated permitted actor; Loan exists, is Active and belongs to that Reader or is being serviced by a Librarian.
- **Success postconditions:** Loan Due Date is extended by 14 calendar days; renewal count/marker records that renewal has been used; the Copy remains Borrowed.
- **Failure guarantee:** An ineligible renewal preserves the original Due Date and renewal marker.

## Main success scenario

1. Reader or Librarian opens the active Loan view and selects Renew.
2. UniLib displays the current Due Date and whether renewal remains available.
3. Actor confirms the request.
4. UniLib begins a transaction and locks the target Loan and title Reservation records needed to decide eligibility.
5. UniLib verifies the Loan is Active, has not passed its Due Date, has never been renewed and has zero pending title Reservations.
6. UniLib sets the Due Date to the previous Due Date plus 14 calendar days and records the one-time renewal.
7. UniLib commits and shows the updated Due Date, with the Renew action disabled for that Loan.

## Alternate and exception flows

- **A1 — Librarian-assisted renewal:** Librarian accesses the Reader's active Loan, passes the same checks and receives the same confirmation.
- **E1 — Already renewed:** Reject and keep current Due Date (BR-03).
- **E2 — Overdue Loan:** Reject; flag Overdue according to FR-F05-07; preserve current Due Date.
- **E3 — Pending title Reservation:** Reject because a waiting Reader has priority; show an actionable message.
- **E4 — Returned or unknown Loan:** Reject with current status / not found; do not reactivate it.
- **E5 — Concurrent reservation placed before commit:** Re-check under transaction serialization/locks; a stale "eligible" badge cannot override a newer pending hold.
- **E6 — Reader attempts another Reader's Loan:** Return forbidden/not found as permitted by the privacy policy, without exposing personal loan data.

## Rules and design constraints

Renewal eligibility depends on the committed loan and reservation state at transaction time. Overdue is determined from the current clock, Due Date and absence of return; it does not necessarily need to be a persisted Loan enum. BR-03 does not state whether a renewal is exactly 14 days from the original due date or request time; the wording "extending ... by 14 days" is interpreted as **old Due Date +14 days**, flagged for BA confirmation. No second renewal is allowed.
