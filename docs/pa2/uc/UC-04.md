# UC-04 — Manage Book Copies & Inventory

**Feature:** F03 (Must) | **Primary actor:** Librarian | **Screen:** SCR-04
**Linked requirements:** FR-F03-01..FR-F03-04, BR-09, BR-10, NFR-05
**Task:** PA2-Q4 | **Owner:** Tran Ha Anh Quan | **Reviewer:** Nguyen / Vinh
**Status:** Ready for peer review | **Registry:** v1.0.0-frozen

## Goal and scope

Register an identified physical Book Copy under an existing Book Title, edit its shelf/condition metadata and decommission an eligible copy without corrupting circulation history. A Book Title is distinct from the individual physical Book Copy.

## Trigger, preconditions and guarantees

- **Trigger:** Librarian opens copy inventory for a selected Book Title.
- **Preconditions:** Authenticated Librarian; Book Title exists; SCR-04 is available.
- **Success postconditions:** A unique barcode/copy ID points to exactly one Book Title; new copy begins Available with a shelf location; metadata changes are persisted; eligible decommission cannot leave a live loan or hold orphaned.
- **Failure guarantee:** Invalid barcodes, missing titles, forbidden state transitions and conflicts do not mutate inventory.

## Main success scenario — Register copy

1. Librarian selects an existing Book Title and opens SCR-04.
2. UniLib displays its registered physical copies, statuses and shelf locations.
3. Librarian selects **Add Copy** and supplies a unique Barcode/Copy ID and Shelf Location.
4. UniLib checks required fields, the Book Title reference and global barcode uniqueness.
5. UniLib creates a Book Copy with lifecycle status **Available**, saving the shelf and optional condition notes.
6. UniLib confirms creation and refreshes inventory counts for the title.

## Alternate and exception flows

- **A1 — Edit metadata:** From step 2, Librarian selects a copy, updates Shelf Location or condition notes, and saves. UniLib validates and persists metadata without changing the circulation state.
- **A2 — Mark non-circulating condition:** A Librarian records Damaged or Lost only through the approved state-control path. A Borrowed or Reserved copy cannot be silently changed into an Available or decommissioned copy. Detailed handling of damage found at check-in remains an open domain decision.
- **A3 — Decommission:** From step 2, Librarian requests decommission. UniLib checks the copy is Available or Damaged/Lost and that no active Loan or assigned Reservation depends on it, then performs the operation and recalculates totals.
- **E1 — Duplicate barcode:** Reject at step 4, show a specific uniqueness error and preserve the form.
- **E2 — Invalid/unknown title:** Reject at step 4 and return to title selection.
- **E3 — Borrowed/Reserved decommission attempt:** Reject before mutation under BR-09 and show a state-conflict warning.
- **E4 — Concurrent circulation change:** If the copy becomes Borrowed/Reserved after the user loaded SCR-04, revalidate under transaction/lock and reject stale decommission; refresh current status.
- **E5 — Unauthorized user:** Enforce JWT/RBAC; Reader cannot create or edit copies.

## Data and business invariants

- Barcode/Copy ID is unique and stable for a physical unit (FR-F03-01).
- Valid copy lifecycle values are Available, Borrowed, Reserved and Damaged/Lost as written in the frozen registry; whether Damaged and Lost are distinct storage values needs approval.
- Circulation operations, not an arbitrary inventory form, transition a copy into Borrowed or Reserved.
- Never decommission Borrowed or Reserved copies (BR-09).
- A Book Title cannot be deleted while any physical copies remain, even when those copies are non-circulating.
